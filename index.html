How the hiring pipeline works and scoring reference

🚀 Quick Start — 3 Steps
1
Add the applicant
Click + New Applicant in the sidebar and fill in their name, email, and position.
2
Send & track
Open Tracker, click their name — a "Next step" banner tells you exactly what to do at every stage.
3
Decide
Once assessments, EMM, and interview are done, record the final decision on their Decision tab.
Pipeline Stages
Every stage is either "TO DO" (something for the team to do) or "WAITING" (nothing to do). Which list an applicant follows depends on their Department.
Track A — AP/AR (Operations)
1
Application Received
Applicant added to the system — already screened before they were entered.
2
TO DO: Send Assessments
HR already interviewed them. Send GRIT + Values, plus EMM if the role requires it.
3
WAITING: Assessments
Test emails sent. Just wait for their answers.
4
TO DO: Interview Them (Ops Manager)
They passed their tests. The Operations Manager needs to interview them.
5
TO DO: Finish Ops Manager Interview
Interview is happening. Write down pass or fail.
6
TO DO: Send to Client
They passed the interview. Send this candidate to the external client now.
7
WAITING: Client’s Decision
Already sent. Just wait for the client to answer.
8
Job Offer Sent
Client approved the candidate; ABBSS sends the formal offer.
9
Hired
Offer accepted.
Track B — Sales & Marketing
1
Application Received
Applicant added to the system — already screened before they were entered.
2
WAITING: Assessments
Test emails sent (GRIT + Values, no EMM on this track). Just wait for their answers.
3
TO DO: Interview Them (HR)
They passed their tests. HR needs to interview them.
4
TO DO: Finish HR Interview
HR is interviewing them. Once done, send to Frances.
5
TO DO: Finish PM Interview
Frances is interviewing them now.
6
TO DO: Finish CEO Interview
The CEO is interviewing them now.
7
Job Offer Sent
CEO approved; ABBSS sends the offer directly — no external client on this track.
8
Hired
Offer accepted.

Recalculate Pipeline Stages

The pipeline was rebuilt with new, more detailed stages. Applicants added before this update kept their old stage number, which now means something different — so some records may show the wrong step (e.g. someone already at the Ops interview showing as still in screening). Run this once to re-check every applicant's actual GRIT/Values/EMM/interview data and correct their stage. It uses your real answers as the source of truth, not the old stored number, and applies the same fix to every applicant automatically — you shouldn't need to fix people one at a time.

Set Up Candidate Stages & Roles

This is the one-time step the Executive Dashboard and per-role Tracker/Board filters need. Existing applicants don't have a Candidate Stage or Role yet, so right now they all show up under "(Unassigned)" instead of split across AR/AP/Refunds/FP&A. This tool reads each applicant's real GRIT/Values/EMM/interview answers to work out their new Candidate Stage, and guesses a Role from their position title (review those afterward — it leaves Role blank rather than guess wrong). It never touches anyone who already has a Candidate Stage set, so it's safe to run more than once. Preview first — nothing changes until you click Apply.

Fix Migration Timestamps

The Candidate Stage migration above used to stamp every record it touched with today's date, which made the Executive Dashboard's "Today's Progress" look like dozens of people moved stages today when really they were just being migrated. That's now fixed going forward — new migrations leave the date honestly blank unless it's a brand-new application (where the real received date is used instead). This tool is the one-time cleanup for records already migrated under the old, inaccurate behavior: it finds any record whose stage date looks fabricated (set to today, with no real history behind it) and clears it, or backfills the true received date if it's a new application. Preview first — nothing changes until you click Apply. Safe to run more than once; once there's nothing left to fix, Preview will say so.
